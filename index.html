life-mode

life-mode is a small personal tool written and used by one person — Panu Wiwattanapa — to organise his own day (a morning plan, an evening wrap-up, and a weekly review).

What it does with Google data

With the owner's own permission, the tool reads his own Gmail account (pookenz@gmail.com) using the read-only scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly, only to show in his private daily summary how many messages are unread and which important messages arrived (sender and subject line only). It never sends, deletes, labels or modifies any message.

Who can use it

Only the owner. It is not offered to the public, there are no other users, and there is nothing to sign up for.
